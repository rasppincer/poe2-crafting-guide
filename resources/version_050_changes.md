## All Omen Changes in 0.5.0

### Disabled/Removed Omens
1. **Omen of Corruption** — Can no longer be obtained
2. **Omen of Recombination** — Has been removed; existing ones deleted upon logging in
3. **Recombinator** — Disabled entirely

### Changed Omens
4. **Omen of Chaotic Rarity** — **FUNCTION INVERTED**: Now prevents modifiers from granting Item Rarity (previously guaranteed they were of that type)
5. **Omen of Chaotic Monsters** — **FUNCTION INVERTED**: Now prevents modifiers from granting Monster Rarity (previously guaranteed they were of that type)
6. **Omen of Chaotic Quantity** — **FUNCTION INVERTED**: Now prevents modifiers from granting Pack Size (previously guaranteed they were of that type)

### New Omens Added
7. **Omen of Chaotic Effectiveness** — **NEW**: Chaos Orb replaces all Waystone modifiers with modifiers that do not grant Monster Effectiveness
8. **Up to 3 Chaotic Omens may be used simultaneously** (was previously only 1)

### Nerfed Omens
9. **Uhtred's Saga** (Uhtred's Omen) — Now grants +2 to level of Supported Skills if exactly one other Support is modifying them (**previously +3**)

### Other Omen Changes
10. **Abyss Omens** — No longer drop in areas below level 65
11. **Ritual Rewards** — All items in Endgame Ritual Reward screen are now either Uniques or Omens
12. **Currency Exchange** — Omen of Corruption, Omen of Homogenising Coronation, and Omen of Homogenising Exaltation only appear on Currency Exchange in Standard Leagues

## Essence Crafting Changes in 0.5.0

### Nerfs
1. **Perfect Essence of Battle** — Now provides +3 to Level of all Attack Skills on Two Hand Melee Weapons or Crossbows (**previously +5**), and +2 to Level of all Attack Skills on One Handed Melee Weapons or Bows (**previously +3**)
2. **Essence of Hysteria** — Now grants 20-23% increased Energy Shield Recharge Rate when applied to Foci (**previously 41-45%**)
3. **Essence Infusion Notable Passive** — Now grants 12% faster start of Energy Shield Recharge, and +12 to Intelligence (previously +10). **No longer grants 40% increased Energy Shield Recharge Rate**
4. **Crystalline Potential Notable Passive** — **Removed entirely**
5. **Base chance for Essences in Maps** — Lowered somewhat due to larger Atlas Tree increases available

### Buffs
6. **Overall Essences in Campaign** — Increased, though proportion of Essences of the Infinite significantly decreased (more useful essences as you progress)
7. **Essence Monster Packs** — Now affected by modifiers to Pack Size

### New Content
8. **Essence of Virtue** — New notable Passive Skill added, grants Virtuous Barrier Skill
9. **Liquid Emotions** — Can now be used to craft additional mods on Jewels, working similarly to greater essences (each has a set of specific mods that replace a random existing mod)
