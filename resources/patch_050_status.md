**Status: ACTIVE (13 alloys available)**

## 2. Desecrated Modifiers in 0.5.0

**Status: STILL ACTIVE - with changes**

### Key 0.5.0 Changes:
- **"All crafted modifiers are now guaranteed, but items can only have 1 crafted modifier at a time."**
- **"Desecrated modifiers no longer count as crafted modifiers, but items are limited to 1 Desecrated modifier."**
- This is a significant change: previously desecrated mods competed with crafted mods for the slot; now they are separate systems but each limited to 1.

### How Desecrated Modifiers Work (unchanged from introduction):
- Added using **Preserved Bone** currency
- Revealed at the **Well of Souls** in Act 2
- Offers choice of 3 modifiers when revealed (at least 1 exclusive modifier on ilvl 65+ items)
- Can reroll options using **Omen of Abyssal Echoes**
- Can only have 1 per item (except via **Omen of Putrefaction** or certain uniques)
- Do NOT count as crafted modifiers (confirmed in 0.5.0)
- Exclusive prefixes: available for most item types except Body Armours, Helmets, Gloves, Boots
- Sceptres have NO exclusive desecrated prefixes or suffixes
- Named modifiers: **Amanamu's**, **Kurgal's**, **Ulaman's** prefixes/suffixes for Abyss-themed mods

### No Changes to Desecrated Modifiers in 0.5.0 Patch Notes
The version history on the wiki page shows the last desecrated modifier changes were in **0.4.0** (amulet Glory modifiers reworked, some removed/updated). No 0.5.0-specific changes listed.

## 3. Expedition / Medved / Amanamu / Kurgal Status

### Expedition League: **TEMPORARILY DISABLED on Standard**
- **"Expedition League has been temporarily disabled on Standard leagues due to the integration with The Runes of Aldur. This will be reintegrated into Standard after The Runes of Aldur is over."**
- The Recombinator has been **disabled**; Omen of Recombination removed and deleted
- "Forgotten By Time" Expedition Precursor Tablet temporarily disabled from dropping
- Legacy Expedition Currency can be sold for Gold
- Rog, Gwennen, Dannig, Tujen converted to non-interactable doodads in hideouts

### Runes of Aldur Replaces Expedition
- Expedition Remnants have been changed into the Remnants from The Runes of Aldur league
- The Logbook mechanic changed into an Ocean Exploring mechanic
- **Medved, Vorana, Uhtred join Olroth** as the 4 Faction Leaders on new Islands
- New endgame story with Farrow exploring the Ocean, investigating Kalguuran tombs

### Amanamu / Kurgal
- Amanamu and Kurgal are **not directly mentioned** in the 0.5.0 patch notes
- They remain as **Abyss-themed desecrated modifier prefixes/suffixes** (Amanamu's, Kurgal's, Ulaman's)
- The **Abyss** mechanic itself is still active with revamped Atlas Tree
- Kulemak's Invitation now always allocated to Map owner
- Abyss Omens no longer drop below level 65

## 4. Other Major Crafting System Changes in 0.5.0

### New Crafting Systems Introduced:
1. **Verisium Runeforging** (Act 1) - Add Runic Ward to armours using Verisium metal
2. **Unique Verisium Runeforging** (Act 3) - Upgrade base types of low-level uniques
3. **Alloys** (Act 2) - 13 new currency items for guaranteed modifier crafting
4. **Ancient Runes** (Act 4) - 13 weapon-type-specific powerful runes
5. **Mythical Runes** - 13 early-game runes for level 15+ characters
6. **Fluxes** - 3 items to transform elemental resistances
7. **Meta Crafting Runes** - 15 new runes
8. **Unique-to-Rune Crafting** - 60+ runes from destroying uniques
9. **Runic Ward Runes** - 15+ runes for Runic Ward properties
10. **Kalguuran Skills** - 23 new skill gems from Remnants
11. **Kalguuran Supports** - 7 new support gems from Remnants
12. **The Genesis Tree** (Breach) - Crafting system using Wombgifts and Hiveblood for rings/amulets/belts
13. **Liquid Emotions** - Craft mods on Jewels (like greater essences)
14. **Potent Emotions** - Powerful jewel modifiers not in normal pool
15. **Ancient Emotions** - For Timelost Jewels
16. **Catalysts** - Now exclusively from Genesis Tree, 12 new jewel quality catalysts

### Crafting Mechanics Changes:
- **Crafted modifiers are now guaranteed** but limited to 1 per item
- Desecrated modifiers separated from crafted modifier count (limited to 1 each)
- Greater/Perfect currencies made rarer; Transmutation/Augmentation significantly rarer
- Greater Orbs of Transmutation/Augmentation min Modifier Level lowered to 44 (was 55)
- Divine Orbs more common
- Corruption "lucky" outcome now multiplies based on current value instead of randomising
- Sanctifying now multiplies modifier values instead of randomising
- **Omen of Corruption can no longer be obtained**
- Essences: more found in campaign overall, but less Essences of the Infinite
- Fate of the Vaal Infusers: 4 types now (Armourer's, Blacksmith's, Arcanist's, Catalysing), require 20% quality
- Vaal Cultivation Orb outcomes adjusted for several uniques

### Disabled/Removed Systems:
- **Recombinator**: Disabled, Omen of Recombination removed
- **Expedition Vendors**: Rog, Gwennen, Dannig, Tujen no longer functional
- **Expedition Currency**: Removed from Currency Exchange (Black Scythe Artifact, Broken Circle Artifact, etc.)
- **Omen of Corruption**: No longer obtainable
